## Seed spread — the bar every comparison must clear

`std_ratio` over 2 seeds: mean **0.502**, sd **0.087**, range 0.441–0.563

`{'v8_s1': 0.44052425026893616, 'v8_s2': 0.5632246732711792}`

These arms share one stage A and differ only in the stage-B seed, so this
is a POLICY-level error bar and understates full run-to-run variance
(paper.md 4m measured an 11.1x fold end to end). **Any arm gap smaller
than this sd is not a result.**

## Design ablations

| comparison | what it isolates |
